Breaking a lock

Every day at work, I lock up my laptop for the night (working with clients means top-secret data!) The lock is a standard 3-digit lock, inset into a cabinet. I always shuffle it after, but I've realized my shuffling strategy isn't the most sophisticated: I just give the digits a push or two, with one finger per digit. This results in a shuffle where each digit has been displaced about the same amount. So I want to figure out whether the original passcode is recoverable from just seeing my shuffled versions. We'll come up with an algorithm to produce an estimate of the original passcode based on a collection of shuffled passcodes. If this algorithm is successful, I'll have to change my shuffling strategy!
